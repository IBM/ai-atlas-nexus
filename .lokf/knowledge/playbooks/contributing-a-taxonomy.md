# Overview

The repository's community-contribution guide, `docs/concepts/Contributing_a_taxonomy.md`, walks through adding your own taxonomies, risks and actions (as LinkML instance YAML under `src/ai_atlas_nexus/data/knowledge_graph/`), adding cross-taxonomy mappings, and adding custom [Chain of Thought templates](../glossary/cot-template.md) for the auto-assist questionnaire and risk-identification features.
