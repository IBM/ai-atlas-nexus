# Overview

AI Atlas Nexus supports custom **CoT templates** for two features: the Auto-assist Risk Questionnaire and Risk Identification (see `docs/concepts/Chain_of_thought_templates.md`). Template data lives under `src/ai_atlas_nexus/data/templates/`; contribution steps are in the [Contributing a Taxonomy or CoT Templates](../playbooks/contributing-a-taxonomy.md) playbook.
